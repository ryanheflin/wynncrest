The Wynncrest process

From manuscript to published book.

Every project is different, but the work typically moves through five connected stages.
01

Assess

Review the manuscript, clarify publication goals, and establish the necessary editorial and production plan.

02

Refine

Complete the manuscript’s publication-ready editorial work, including copyediting, proofreading, and final author review.

03

Produce

Design the cover and interior, typeset print editions, prepare ebooks, and perform production quality control.

04

Publish

Prepare metadata, ISBNs, files, print and digital editions, distribution, and publication infrastructure.

05

Extend

Build the launch and long-term strategy around the role the book should play in the author's business, platform, mission, or body of work.
