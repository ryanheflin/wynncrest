The Wynncrest process

One team from first idea to finished book.

Every project is different, but the work typically moves through five connected stages.
01

Develop

Clarify the concept, audience, positioning, structure, purpose, competitive landscape, and plan for the book.

02

Create

Write, ghostwrite, develop, or refine the manuscript with editorial guidance appropriate to the project.

03

Produce

Move through editing, proofreading, cover and interior design, typesetting, ebook preparation, and quality control.

04

Publish

Prepare metadata, ISBNs, files, print and digital editions, distribution, and publication infrastructure.

05

Extend

Build the launch and long-term strategy around the role the book should play in the author's business, platform, mission, or body of work.
