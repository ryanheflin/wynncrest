Open with a direct answer to the question the article is addressing. A reader—and a search or AI system—should be able to understand the central answer from the first few paragraphs.

## Use specific, descriptive headings

Explain the subject in clear language. Prefer concrete examples, distinctions, and definitions over vague marketing claims.

## Answer likely follow-up questions

A strong Wynncrest Guide article should help a reader understand what to do next, what tradeoffs exist, and when professional help may be useful.

## Keep the source clear

Write from Wynncrest's publishing experience. Avoid unsupported statistics and exaggerated claims. Link to relevant Wynncrest service pages where they genuinely help the reader.
