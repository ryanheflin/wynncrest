What we look for

Work worth doing well.

We are drawn to books with a clear reason to exist: useful ideas, hard-won expertise, compelling stories, and work capable of creating connection, growth, hope, understanding, or meaningful change.

We also care about execution. A meaningful idea deserves rigorous editorial work, considered design, professional production, and a publishing strategy that respects both the author and the reader.
