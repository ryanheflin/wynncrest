About Wynncrest

A publishing house built around care, craft, and trust.

Wynncrest Books is the independent publishing imprint of Heflin Media Group, based in the United States. We partner with authors whose ideas, expertise, and stories deserve to be developed with exceptional care.

Our model combines the freedom and speed of independent publishing with the editorial, design, production, and strategic standards expected of an experienced publishing team.

Founder

Liz Heflin

Founder, Wynncrest Books · President, Heflin Media Group

Liz Heflin has worked as a nonfiction ghostwriter and content marketing consultant since 2006, helping high achievers in business and entertainment turn their experience, ideas, and stories into books designed to make an impact.

Her work sits at the intersection of writing, publishing strategy, and marketing. She believes a strong manuscript is only one part of the job: authors also need a publishing path and a plan for helping the right readers discover the finished book.

Wynncrest grew from that philosophy. Rather than forcing every author into the same process, we build experienced teams around the needs of each book and work collaboratively from the earliest idea through publication.

What guides us

The way we want publishing to feel.

01

High-touch

Authors should understand where their project stands, what comes next, and who is responsible for getting it there.

02

Carefully built teams

We select writers, editors, designers, and publishing professionals for the needs of the project rather than forcing every book through a standardized package.

03

Quality without shortcuts

Our commitment is to create a finished book that represents its author and ideas professionally at every stage.

04

Relentless integrity

We believe in candid advice, realistic expectations, and services that genuinely serve the book—not exaggerated promises or unnecessary upsells.

05

Books with something to add

We are drawn to stories and ideas that foster hope, growth, connection, understanding, inclusion, and meaningful positive change.
