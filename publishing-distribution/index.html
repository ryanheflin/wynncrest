Independent publishing

Control and professional standards can coexist.

Independent publishing can give an author greater control over timing, rights, creative decisions, and strategy. The tradeoff is that the author needs a capable publishing infrastructure. Wynncrest exists to provide that infrastructure without forcing every book through the same model.
Common questions

What authors usually want to know.

The right publishing path depends on where the manuscript stands, what the book needs to accomplish, and how much of the process the author wants to manage personally.
01

Is Wynncrest a traditional publisher?

Wynncrest is an independent publishing imprint and publishing partner. Projects are structured differently from conventional trade-publishing acquisitions, and the scope is built around the needs of the book and author.

02

Will the book be available through retailers?

Distribution options depend on the project, edition, territory, and strategy. Wynncrest can set up print and digital distribution appropriate to the intended market.

03

Who controls the book?

Rights, responsibilities, financial terms, and control are defined in the publishing agreement for each project. Wynncrest’s model is designed around clarity and author involvement rather than one-size-fits-all assumptions.
