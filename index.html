Questions authors ask

You can start before you have all the answers.

Authors come to Wynncrest at very different stages. These are some of the questions that most often determine what kind of publishing support a project needs next.
01

Can I come to Wynncrest with only an idea?

Yes. If your manuscript is not yet written, Heflin Media Group, our parent company, can help develop the concept and writing plan. Wynncrest takes the lead when the book is ready for publishing preparation.

02

Do you provide ghostwriters?

Our parent company, Heflin Media Group, provides ghostwriting and manuscript creation. Wynncrest specializes in editorial finishing, book design, production, publishing, and distribution.

03

What if my manuscript is already finished?

Completed manuscripts can enter at the editorial, design, production, or publishing stage after we evaluate what the book needs before release.

04

Is Wynncrest a traditional publisher?

Wynncrest is an independent publishing imprint and publishing partner. Projects are structured around the needs and goals of the author and book rather than a conventional trade-publishing acquisition model.

05

Where can I learn about the process before contacting you?

The Wynncrest Guide to Publishing explains writing, ghostwriting, editing, production, publishing, distribution, and author strategy in practical language.
