Editorial & Production

A finished draft is the beginning of the publishing work.

Once a manuscript exists, it still has to become a professional book. Wynncrest coordinates editorial refinement, proofreading, design, typesetting, ebook preparation, production files, and quality control as one connected process.
Editorial stages

Different manuscripts need different kinds of editing.

Editing is not one task. The right sequence depends on what the manuscript needs and how close it is to publication.
01

Developmental editing

Address structure, argument, pacing, chapter function, omissions, repetition, and the overall reader experience.

02

Line & copy editing

Improve clarity, consistency, style, grammar, usage, factual presentation, and sentence-level effectiveness.

03

Proofreading

Review the near-final typeset book for typographical errors, formatting issues, and production-stage mistakes.

04

Author review & quality control

Manage queries, approvals, corrections, and handoffs so changes survive correctly from manuscript to final files.

Design & production

A professional manuscript deserves a professional object.

Cover design, interior typography, trim size, page architecture, image handling, print specifications, and ebook formatting all shape the reader’s experience. Production should serve the book rather than call attention to itself.
Common questions

What authors usually want to know.

The right publishing path depends on where the manuscript stands, what the book needs to accomplish, and how much of the process the author wants to manage personally.
01

Can Wynncrest edit a manuscript that is already complete?

Yes. A completed manuscript can enter at the appropriate editorial stage after an evaluation of what it needs before production.

02

Do I need separate vendors for editing, cover design, and typesetting?

Not when Wynncrest is managing the production process. We assemble and coordinate the appropriate specialists around the project.

03

Can you produce both print and ebook editions?

Yes. Production can include print-ready files and digital editions appropriate to the project and distribution plan.
