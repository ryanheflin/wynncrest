Common questions

What authors usually want to know.

The right publishing path depends on where the manuscript stands, what the book needs to accomplish, and how much of the process the author wants to manage personally.
01

Do I need to write the manuscript myself?

No. Some leaders write extensively; others work through interviews and collaborative ghostwriting. The right model depends on the author’s time, writing preferences, material, and goals.

02

Can the book support my business without feeling promotional?

Yes. The most credible business books are useful on their own terms. Strategy can connect the book to a larger platform without turning the manuscript into an advertisement.

03

Can organizations commission books too?

Yes. Organizations may have histories, methodologies, research, missions, or institutional knowledge that can be developed into professionally published books.
